Music - what it stands on

A music player in the system's own look. It decodes nothing and filters nothing: songs are read and decoded by media.lua and the decoding kits, the equaliser and the meter are the audio server's, per stream, and the library is a kit Video and the Open window can use too. Music itself is Lua - what a person does - and the thinnest layer there is.

P - a period of a song

  1. P1 media.lua's feed decodes - minimp3, the FLAC Kit, or a WAV's samples - into the stream's ring, as far ahead as the ring allows. C; no Lua string a period.
  2. P2 The server mixes the stream with gain and balance, as now.
  3. P3 Its equaliser, if on: ten biquads a channel, coefficients worked out when a setting arrives, never in the mix. Off, it costs a comparison.
  4. P4 Its peak, a channel each, kept for the next streams reply.
  5. P5 The period to the device.

Music's window is not on this path: it asks the server ten times a second for the meter and the position, and paints a strip.
