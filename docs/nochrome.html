Windows without title bars

Drawn on 27 September 2026, before any of it is built — roadmap.md 6zj. Diego: “it just opens directly with the content and the dragging occurs directly from the app itself”, and “buttons on the right”. The window buttons move into the application's own header, at its right end, and the header's empty band is what a press drags. Today's bar is drawn as wm.lua draws it, for comparison.

Tracker

The same window, 26 pixels shorter. On the right the header ends with the three, 10 pixels after the last of its own buttons and 12 from the edge — the header's own right margin. The sidebar's head and the header are still one band across the window, so the whole top edge is somewhere to take hold of.

Preferences and the Kosmos IDE

Preferences' header has only its page's name, so almost all of it drags. The IDE's has the most buttons of any window; the three sit after them, and the band between the file's name and the buttons is the handle.

Which window is in front

Today the tab says it: the front window's tab is the look's colour, every other one grey. With no tab, the buttons say it — coloured on the window in front, grey on the rest — and the front window's shadow is the deeper one. It is the Mac's answer, and it is the part of a tab that was doing the work.

What keeps a title bar

A window with no header of the kit's has nothing to put the buttons in or to take hold of: the Cube, Gears, Doom, Quake, the Super Nintendo — windows that draw their own pixels. They keep today's bar, in every look. And the BeOS look keeps its yellow tab on every window, since the tab is what BeOS is; content-first is a property of the look, and the Plex looks have it.

The title does not go away — only its bar. A window is still named by its title in the Deskbar, in Super+Tab and in /Running; it is just not drawn above the window a second time.

To decide

  2. The header's empty band drags the window, and a double click on it maximises, as a title bar's did.

    Buttons, fields and the trail keep their clicks; only what has nothing else to do is handle.

  3. Which window is in front is said by the buttons: coloured in front, grey behind, and a deeper shadow on the front one.
  4. Windows that draw their own pixels keep today's bar in every look.
  5. It is the look's choice: the Plex looks content-first, the BeOS look keeping its tab.
