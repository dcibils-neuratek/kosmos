What a person can do

Sign in

A Google account with its address and an app password, or any IMAP account with its server shown - checked before it is kept, the password in the keyring.

Read

Mailboxes by what they are, counts beside them; conversations kept together; HTML as its sender drew it, plain text set by Write's engine.

Answer

Reply, Reply All, Forward; To, Cc and Bcc completed as they are typed; drafts kept as they are written; sent and kept in Sent.

Tidy

Read or unread, flag, archive, delete, move - from the tools, the keys and the right button, on one or several.

Find

Search by who, subject and words as they are typed, in a mailbox or all - the system's search, since a message is a file with its facts as attributes.

Know

New mail as it is sent (IMAP's IDLE), a banner for each, with the window open or closed.

Yours to decide

  1. Two programs: the window, and maild running from when the desktop starts, fetching and notifying with the window closed. Recommended: two - a banner with the window closed is what you asked for.
  2. maild Mail's own, or a system server any application sends mail through. Recommended: Mail's own, until a second application needs to send mail.
  3. Plain text first in the composer, rich text once Write's typing is moved out of writer.lua. Recommended: plain text first.
  4. The keyring's door for mail lent only to the system's Mail, so a copy made in the IDE asks for its password each time. Recommended: yes.
  5. Opening 10,000 messages in a frame: a mailbox's own list file, or the attributes alone. Recommended: measure when the window is built, and decide then.
  6. All Inboxes and conversations in the first version, as drawn. Recommended: in it.
  7. The three columns, as drawn - mailboxes, list, message - with the mailboxes hidden by the button at the header's left on a small screen. Recommended: as drawn.
