Kosmos Logo Concepts

Six ideas for a Kosmos mark. Each is shown large, as a tile on the dock, and at 16 points beside the name, on Night and on a light ground. Pick one, or parts of two, and it gets refined, then built in 3D to turn beside the KOSMOS letters.

"we can also invent a logo in 3d that rotates and else", "We need a logo for kosmos" - Diego, 8 October

What the name gives to work with: kosmos is Greek for order, and then for the ordered universe; the kernel is Nebula, a cloud stars are born from; the desktop is Astra, the stars. A microkernel is a small core with everything else around it, asked by message.

Kosmos Kosmos
1 · ORBIT

A world and its ring

A planet with a tilted ring and one small moon on it: the universe in the simplest shape anyone reads at once. Quiet and certain, and it stays a planet at 16 points.

In 3D
The planet turns, the moon travels the ring; a light circling makes the ring's shadow cross the sphere.
Risk
Planets with rings are common marks; it says "space" more than it says "Kosmos".
Kosmos Kosmos
2 · CORE

The microkernel, drawn

A small bright core - Nebula - with three servers close around it and more in the outer ring: what Kosmos actually is, a little kernel and everything else around it, asked. It also reads as an atom or a solar system.

In 3D
The three spheres orbit the core on tilted paths, each a different speed; the outer ring of points turns slowly the other way.
Risk
Four colours is busy at 16 points; the small one could be the core and three dots alone.
Kosmos Kosmos
3 · CONSTELLATION

A K written in stars

Seven stars joined into a K, the way a constellation is a shape found in scattered points: order out of the sky, which is the word's own meaning. The brightest star is where the strokes meet.

In 3D
Stars at different depths that only line up into a K from the front: the camera swings round, the K falls apart into scattered points, and gathers again.
Risk
At 16 points the stars drop out and it is a plain K, which is the fallback drawn.
Kosmos Kosmos
4 · BLOCK

A cube with a K cut in

An isometric cube, a K cut into its left face and a little window on its right: a building block, the kits and servers an application is put together from. It is already solid, so it is the one that is most natural in 3D, and it shares the pencil letters' block look.

In 3D
The cube turns on a corner and the light going round the KOSMOS letters lights its three faces in turn, the K's cut throwing its own shadow.
Risk
Cubes are a common mark for software; the cut K is what makes it ours, and it vanishes small.
Kosmos Kosmos
5 · ASTRA

A star, being born

A four-pointed star in a soft glow, and a small one beside it: Astra, the desktop, out of Nebula, the cloud. The simplest of the six, and the one that reads best at 16 points and on a dock.

In 3D
The star as a faceted crystal turning slowly, catching the circling light on its points; the glow breathes.
Risk
Sparkles are what every AI product chose in 2024 and 2025; it would need the second star or a cut of its own to be ours.
Kosmos Kosmos
6 · NEBULA

A galaxy unwinding

One line winding out from a bright centre into a spiral, a single star loose at its end: a galaxy forming, the kernel at the middle and everything growing from it. One stroke, so it is easy to draw anywhere.

In 3D
The spiral as a disc of points seen at a tilt, turning; the arm catches the light as it comes round.
Risk
A spiral can read as a loading spinner or a snail; the star at its end is what keeps it a galaxy.

Yours to choose

  1. Which one, or which two to combine - the Block's cube with the Constellation's K on its face, the Orbit's ring around the Core. My pick: 4, Block, for being ours, solid in 3D and matching the block letters; 2, Core, for saying what Kosmos is.
  2. One colour or several. A mark in Night's pale blue alone works on every look; the Core's three colours are the new looks' accents. Recommended: one colour, with the others kept for the 3D showcase.
  3. Where it goes first: the dock's Kosmos button, the About window, the boot screen, the launcher's power row. Recommended: all four, once it is chosen and refined.
