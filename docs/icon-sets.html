Icon sets

One set of line icons for every button bar in Kosmos - New, Open, Save, Copy, Run, and the rest - chosen by eye. Each is shown in the Kosmos IDE's bar at the size the kit draws them, 15 pixels, on a light and a dark window, then every icon at 30 pixels (the desktop at 200 per cent) and 15, in grey, with Save in the accent as a chosen one would be. The dashed squares are icons Kosmos does not have yet.

Whichever is chosen is carried the way Kosmos's own icons already are: the set's SVGs are the source, tools/lineicons.py renders each at 15, 19, 23 and 30 pixels, and the PNGs keep only coverage - white with its transparency - so the kit paints them grey, or the accent, in every look, light or dark (gc:line_icon).

Yours to choose

  • Lucide is proposed: it is the closest to what the drawings have been drawing by hand - the same even line with round ends - and it holds up at 15 pixels.
  • Tabler is the same idea with more icons in it and a slightly squarer hand; Phosphor's light weight is the finest, and at 15 pixels its lines are thinner than the rest of the window's.
  • Kosmos's 26 hand-drawn ones would be replaced by the chosen set's, so no window mixes two hands.
