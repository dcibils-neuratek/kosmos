Kosmos Music

Music in the system's own look: Tracker's sidebar, the header as the title bar, the theme every window shares. MP3, WAV and FLAC; songs as now and albums as covers; a small meter beside what is playing; and an equaliser one button away.

Diego, 11 October 2026: "lets redesign the music app with a more native music app with the ui style of kosmos", "it needs to support mp3, wav, flac", "album view as well", "song view as now", "a small uv meter", "equializer", "make it look nice and native for kosmos".

Songs, as Music opens

What is playing stays on top, as now: the cover, the song, its artist and album, its format and quality, the transport, and a bar you can drag. On the right, the meter: left and right as two rows of twenty lamps, green to −12 dB, yellow to −3, red above, the highest lamp of the last second held as a peak. Under it the volume and the equaliser's button, which says On when it is. The list is today's, with Format as a column of its own; the song playing has three moving bars where its number was.

Albums

Every album in the library as its cover, from the file's own picture: an MP3's tag, FLAC's picture block, or a cover.jpg beside the songs. An album with no picture gets one drawn from its name's letters on a colour, so the grid never has holes. The album playing is outlined.

An album, opened

One click on a cover. The cover large, the album's facts beneath it, Play and Shuffle, and its songs in order. ‹ Albums, or Escape, goes back.

The equaliser

Ten bands an octave apart, 32 Hz to 16 kHz, each ±12 dB, with a preamp to keep a boost from clipping. The curve behind the sliders is what the ten together do. Presets: Flat, Bass, Treble, Vocal, Rock, Classical, Late night, and what you made yourself, kept by name. On or off with the switch; the button in the header remembers which. It shapes what Music plays and nothing else.

What it is made of, before the architecture is drawn

  • The window is the UI kit's: ui.sidebar, the header as the title bar, a list, a grid of cards. Nothing drawn in a palette of its own, which is the point of the redesign.
  • Playing, seeking and the meter are media.lua's, as now. The meter wants left and right, where the audio server publishes one peak a stream today: a second number in the stream's ring, beside the first.
  • The equaliser is a kit, not Music's. Video, Groove and the Mixer all want one, so it is C in the sound path: ten biquad filters a channel over each period, in the vector unit, set by a message and never by a sample crossing Lua. Music is its first user.
  • FLAC goes through FFmpeg, which the Video app already links; its tags and picture through the tag reader that reads MP3's.
  • Albums are the library grouped by the album tag, with the covers decoded once and kept small.
