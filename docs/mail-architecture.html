Kosmos Mail - what it stands on, and what the next version adds

A native mail client: IMAP and SMTP in Lua over the TLS Kit, a message's bytes undone in C by the Mail Kit, and maild fetching and announcing new mail with the window closed. The next version adds conversations, rules and the server's search to maild, and a styled composer, spelling and contacts from the office kits (docs/office.md).

A - a message arriving

  1. A1 The server says * 18433 EXISTS on the connection held in IDLE.
  2. A2 maild fetches it into its file as it arrives.
  3. A3 The Mail Kit parses it once, from a region.
  4. A4 Its facts written as attributes, with its conversation's key (mailthreads).
  5. A5 The rules run (mailrules): moved, flagged, read, junk, or no banner.
  6. A6 A banner, unless a rule said none.
  7. A7 The window told by a post, and the line added to its conversation.
