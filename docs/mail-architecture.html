A - a message arriving

  1. A1 The server says * 18433 EXISTS on the connection maild holds in IDLE.
  2. A2 maild leaves IDLE and fetches the message, copied from the connection into its file as it arrives.
  3. A3 The Mail Kit parses it once, from a region.
  4. A4 Its facts - from, subject, date, preview, seen - written as the file's attributes.
  5. A5 A banner posted through the notifications.
  6. A6 The window told, by a post, and adds one line; maild goes back to IDLE.

Most of the time is the network's. Nothing waits on the window, and the window waits on nothing.
