Journeys

    Two ways to cross

    messageA call to another process, through Nebula: what to do. Small, copied, a declared shape.
    regionPages two processes share: what to do it to. Pixels, samples, file bytes - never copied as messages.
    callCode inside the same process: a kit or a library.

    The rules that shape it

    What you were not handed, you cannot reach

    A process sees only the paths its namespace was given, each a capability: an index into its own table. No global names, no ambient authority.

    Control by message, data by shared memory

    A message says what to do; a region holds what to do it to. Frames, sound periods, packets and disk blocks never travel as messages.

    C for what others wait on, Lua for what a person decides

    The kernel, drivers, servers and the byte loops of kits are C. Applications, the window manager's policy and libraries are Lua.

    Nothing waits on hardware

    A driver hands its hardware work and collects the result when the hardware says. A window draws what it already heard and asks on its own clock.

    Kits supply, applications orchestrate

    Anything another app could want is a kit with one door. An application is the thinnest layer: what a person does, put together from what the system supplies.

    Sound is above everything

    The audio band sits over the desktop's, and a thread there that runs too long without sleeping drops back. Sound keeps playing under load.
