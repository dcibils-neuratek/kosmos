The office kits - what Mail, Write, Sheets and Present share

Spelling, find and replace, a rich-text editor, contacts, templates, kept copies and Office's files, each a kit with one door, built once by whichever application needs it first. Every kit runs in its user's own process: no new server, no new system call.

Asked of every piece

  1. Does another application want this? Each kit here has two users or more from the start.
  2. Does something already supply this? textbuf:find, pdf.text, markup.lua's escaper, recentdocs, addresses, expat in the tree, gfx.encode_png - each used rather than written again.

The order

  1. O1 the moves out of Write, Write's suites unchanged
  2. O2 find and replace
  3. O3 the Spelling Kit, English and Spanish
  4. O4 kept copies, previews, templates
  5. O5 richhtml - Mail's styled composer
  6. O6 the People Kit - Mail's contacts
  7. O7 the XML Kit, ooxml read - DOCX opened

The busiest path - a key that ends a word

  1. textedit makes a new body, sharing every paragraph it did not touch.
  2. pageset sets that paragraph again.
  3. The Spelling Kit looks the word up: one walk of the graph, microseconds.
  4. pagedraw draws the page, the wave as one more mark.

Measured in Write at O1, by stage and by what each allocates; every step after is held to it.
