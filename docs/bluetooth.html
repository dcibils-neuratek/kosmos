Bluetooth

One window to turn Bluetooth on, see what is near, add a keyboard or a mouse, and look after the ones that are yours. Drawn on 20 October 2026, before any of it is built; docs/bluetooth.md has what it is made of. For Diego to change until it is right.

Diego, 20 October: "Also we neeed a Bluetooth app to add and manage devices".

The window, scanning

My devices are the ones paired, connected or not, each with its battery where it reports one. Nearby is what is pairable around the machine, nearest first, while the window is open; scanning stops when it closes. The line under the header is the adapter, as the log says it at B1. The switch in the header turns the radio off, and every device with it.

Adding a keyboard

A keyboard proves it is the one in front of you: the six digits are typed on it, and each one fills in as it is typed - the keyboard reports every keypress while it pairs, though the keys reach no window. Return finishes. A mouse has no keys to type on, so it is added with no sheet at all, and the row reads Adding and then Connected.

A device's menu

The ··· on a row, or a right click on it. Disconnect lets it go until it wakes and asks again; Forget removes its keys, and it must be added again.

Off

Off says what that means and nothing else. With no adapter plugged in the window says so, and how to get one, in the same place.
