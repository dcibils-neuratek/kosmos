PDF Viewer - what it stands on

The reader is the window: layout, scrolling, zoom, the sidebar, Find's marks. What a PDF is - its pages, its text, its outline, its pictures - is the PDF Kit's, behind its one door pdf.lua, so Present, Sheets, the browser and Kosmos Write get each addition too. Scrolling is a blit of pages already rendered; only a page arriving in view is drawn.

S - a page scrolled into view

  1. S1 the reader's layout says page 213 is about to show and is not in the cache.
  2. S2 pdf.render: its content stream read and tokenised in C, the operators run in Lua.
  3. S3 its glyphs drawn from the cache, its pictures decoded and placed - C - into a surface at the zoom.
  4. S4 every frame after: the visible band of each page in view blitted to the window, C, nothing redrawn.
