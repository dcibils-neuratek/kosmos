Automations - what it stands on

The window only shows and edits automations. A background program, autod, runs them on their triggers, each step a verb from a library any program can use. A step that presses a button reaches the application through the UI kit, which publishes every control by name. That is the larger new piece, and it makes every application scriptable.

the scheduler's clock

autod asleep until the nearest time

T - a trigger fires

  1. T1 autod wakes: the time came, a listing it watches changed, a stick appeared in /Drives, or maild said a mail arrived.
  2. T2 each step in turn, a call into automate.lua; what a step found passed to the next.
  3. T3 a line of history a step, and a banner if it failed.

P - a step presses a button

  1. P1 press on /Running/mail/controls/Get Mail: one message, which only a holder of Mail's name may send.
  2. P2 Mail's kit runs the button's own click handler, so the press does exactly what a click does.
