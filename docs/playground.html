Devices

One window with a page for every device this machine has: what it says right now, something to do with it, and the few lines of Lua that reach it. The page is the demonstration and the code is the lesson.

A drawing, not a program. Diego's idea, 19 September: "a device playground app to interact with recognized hardware". Only devices the machine really has are listed - on QEMU there is no battery page, and a pad appears when one is plugged in.

The left meter moves further than the right because the balance is to the left: the page shows the change it makes, which is the point Diego named - "moving the balance or volume meter and seeing how it changes the sound".
