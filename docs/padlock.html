JetSki's padlock and shield

Two things in JetSki's toolbar that say what a page is. The padlock, at the head of the address, says how the page came and whether its certificate checked out. The shield, beside the address, says what Privacy and Ad Blocking kept out: this page's, and all of it since JetSki was first used. Drawn on 16 November 2026, before either is built. The shield's pop-over and its two per-site switches are agreed already in adblock.html; what is new here is the padlock, the page for a refused certificate, and the totals.

Diego: "its lacking a ssl certificate and safe page indicator like we did with the old browser", "we need to show how many ads are blocked, and cookies, and else somewhere in jetski", "i want a button that i can press and disable ad blocking and else on a per website basis".

A page of JetSki's own in place of the site, with the reason in the network process's words. Open anyway loads the site for this tab only and is remembered nowhere; the tab then says Not secure for as long as it stays on that site, the old browser's rule. Nothing from a refused certificate is kept in the cache, and none of its cookies are kept.

The pop-over agreed in adblock.html, with one band added: everything kept out since JetSki was first used, across every tab and site. The totals are kept in a small file in /Home/Settings/Filters, written when a page finishes, so they survive a restart. The two switches are the per-site button Diego asked for. The first turns blocking off for this site alone and reloads it. The second lets this site's cross-site cookies through. Either one fixes a site that breaks. The numbers are examples.

Decided by Diego, 16 November

  • Open anyway: kept ("Yes, open anyway"), as the old browser had it - for this tab, remembered nowhere, and the tab says Not secure while it stays on that site.
  • Secure is a green padlock alone ("yes, secure as with a green lock"), no word, as Safari and Chrome show it.
  • Totals by month ("Let's do totals by month"): the shield's band and Settings' row say this month's - "This month, every site" - and start again on the first of each month, every month kept in the file so earlier months can be listed. No Start again button is needed.
