Banners and alerts

Two kinds, as the picture above has them. A banner - a render done, a stick arrived, a download finished - comes in at the top right under the strip, stays five seconds, and goes; a press on it opens what sent it. An alert - a program that stopped, a reminder, a timer - stays until it is closed, because it is the kind that is missed if it goes by itself. Several stack, newest on top. Both are kept in the history.

The history

Opened from the clock in the strip, as macOS and Googlebook open theirs; a dot beside the date says something arrived since it was last looked at. Grouped by what sent it, newest first, each with when; a press opens it, and Clear all empties it. With the bar at the top, the clock at its right end opens the same panel.

How an application says something

One call, from any application or program; the server knows who sent it from the message itself, so nothing can speak for another. What crosses is a declared shape (notifyproto.h), as every server here speaks.

local notify = use("/Kosmos/Libraries/notify.lua")

notify.post{ title = "Render finished",
             body  = "Kitchen.c3d, 1920 by 1080, in 4 min 12 s.",
             alert = false,                   -- true: it stays until closed
             open  = "/Home/Renders" }        -- what a press on it opens

What is yours to decide

1. Where the history opens

From the clock in the strip, as drawn, or from a bell of its own beside the indicators.

The clock: where macOS and Googlebook keep it, and the strip stays as quiet as it is.

2. Banners and alerts

Two kinds - a banner goes after five seconds, an alert (a program that stopped, a reminder, a timer) stays until closed - or one kind that always goes.

Two, as drawn: an alert is the kind that is missed if it leaves by itself.

3. A sound

A short sound with each notification, or silence.

Silence for now, and a sound per application in its row later.

4. Do Not Disturb: a switch, or a schedule too

Only the switch, as drawn, or also hours of its own (23:00 to 7:00).

The switch now, the hours later.
