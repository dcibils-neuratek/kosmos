Wallpapers

A folder of your own, /Home/Wallpapers; any picture chosen from anywhere; and every one seen as a small picture before it is chosen, in a row of cards along Preferences' Appearance page.

The Appearance page

The Wallpaper row is a card of its own: None first, the desk's own colour; then the pictures in /Home/Wallpapers, newest first, each under its name; then the photographs Kosmos carries, under their photographers' names; and last, Choose a picture... as a card - which is also a button at the top right, so it is there whichever way the row is scrolled. One click sets it at once - the desk changes behind the window while it is open - and the card is outlined and ticked. The row scrolls by the wheel, by dragging, or by its arrows, and opens scrolled to the one in use. Wallpaper size stays beneath it.

Choose a picture...

The Open window, showing pictures only, starting in Photos. Use as wallpaper sets it and copies it into /Home/Wallpapers, so it is in the row next time and stays there when the original is moved or deleted - the folder is the whole list, and a person can see and tidy it in Tracker. A picture already in the folder is not copied again.

In Tracker

Wallpapers joins the sidebar's places, beside Photos, so the folder is somewhere to drop pictures by dragging as well as by choosing. It is made the first time Kosmos starts with a /Home that has none - empty, so nothing is put there on a person's behalf.

What it is made of

  • The list is settings.wallpapers, as now, reading /Home/Wallpapers where it read the top of /Home. A picture there from before still works, as a chosen file is a path.
  • The cards' pictures are thumbnails the window manager makes once a picture - decoded at 150 by 94, the size they are drawn, and kept while Preferences is open - through the same door ui.image uses. Twenty full photographs decoded at their own size to be drawn the size of a stamp would be most of a second and most of a gigabyte.
  • The row of cards is a kit piece, ui.carousel: a strip of cards that scrolls, one chosen. The Shortcuts app's sections and Music's albums on a narrow window want the same.
  • Choose a picture... is the Open window (docs/drives.html), filtered to pictures.
