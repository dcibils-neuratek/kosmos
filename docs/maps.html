What a person can do

Look around

Drag to move, the wheel or a pinch to zoom, Ctrl and a drag to turn the map; the compass puts north back up. Explore, Satellite later, Transit later.

Find a place

Search for a place, a street or an address; results in the sidebar and as pins on the map. A pressed pin or label opens its card: name, kind, distance, hours and address.

Keep places

Pinned along the top - Home, Work and any others - Saved below them, and Recently viewed. Kept by the settings kit, on this machine only.

Directions, later

A route drawn on the map, by car, on foot or by bicycle, with its steps in the sidebar - from OSRM or Valhalla, a step after the map itself.

Without a network

A region kept on the disk as one PMTiles file - a city, a country - and the map works with no network at all.

Night and Light

The map takes the window's look: the night's blue-greys, or a light map with its parks green and water blue, as the toggle above shows.

Yours to decide

  1. A sidebar always there, as drawn, or the map filling the window with the sidebar a button away. Recommended: always there, hidden with Ctrl+S on a small screen.
  2. The place card over the map, at its top left as Apple's floats, or inside the sidebar in place of the lists. Recommended: over the map, as drawn.
  3. Where the map comes from: OpenFreeMap's tiles over the network first, a PMTiles region on the disk second. Recommended: both, the network first.
  4. Satellite and Transit drawn as switches now and greyed until built, or left out until they exist. Recommended: left out until they exist.

The architecture and its diagram come next, once the window is agreed: the Map Kit in C that decodes a tile and draws it, the tiles fetched through the TLS Kit and kept under /Home, and Lua for what a person does.
