Privacy and Ad Blocking, in JetSki

Adverts and trackers kept out of every page, on WebKit's own content blocker, the engine Safari's blockers run on. The public filter lists AdBlock and Adblock Plus use are converted into its rules by a Kosmos library, and third-party cookies are refused. Drawn on 16 November 2026, before it is built; docs/adblock.md is the feature set and the architecture.

Diego: "We need a way to have adblocking plugin for browsing to avoid loading and saving in unnecessary data", "I want to be able to avoid tracking cookies and else".

The shield sits in the toolbar before the star and counts what was kept out of this page as it loads: green with a number while blocking is on. Pressed, it opens the pop-over: requests kept out, cookies refused, an estimate of the bytes not downloaded (from the size each blocked address gave last time it was let through, or the median for its kind), and the sites blocked most. Block adverts and trackers off reloads the page with blocking off for that site alone; nothing large is compiled again, so it is instant. Allow cross-site cookies is the site's own cookie switch (Diego, 16 November): on, third parties' cookies are kept while this site is the page - for a sign-in spread across domains - and every other site still refuses them. Where an advert's box would have been, the page closes up: EasyList's element-hiding rules hide the empty slot. The page, its numbers and its sites are examples; the sites are among those that set cookies on the M700 during The Verge's load on 15 November.

Decided by Diego, 16 November

The name: Privacy and Ad Blocking.
Default lists: EasyList, EasyPrivacy and EasyList Cookie, all on - "yes".
Acceptable Ads: offered, off - "offer it to switch on and off but default is we dont show any banners".
The trackers' cookies cleared by themselves, and cookies allowed one site at a time - "clear them and have a way to opt out for cookies on a site-basis".
