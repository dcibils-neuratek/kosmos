Three directions, and the earlier proposal for comparison. Press the sound or bell icon in the strip for quick settings; the buttons play each direction's motion. Nothing here is built. The three directions

Each is a set of signature cues - a title shape, a colour, a material, a Deskbar and dock, an icon style and one motion. Every cue is priced for the scene in C on the processor, where composing measures 3.4 ns a pixel on the M700 (testing.md 18.657). Green is what each costs.

Starlight CHOSEN, AS A LOOK

Astra means stars. BeOS's "the focused one is the yellow one", carried into the header Diego chose.

The plate
The title of the window in front sits on an amber plate at the header's left, its right edge cut at 45 degrees; behind, the plate is the header's own tone. The window's top-left corner is square where the plate meets it. A trapezoid fill, ~9 kpx, on a change of front only.
Colour
Flare amber (tab) on night indigo; astral blue for selection. A light look keeps the amber and the indigo ink. Tokens: nothing.
Material: nebula glass
Wallpaper glass tinted by a gradient, with star-dust grain, and a one-pixel amber horizon along its top. Gradient and grain baked into the blurred wallpaper once; the horizon is a 1 px line.
The cut, used sparingly
One small cut at the top-left of the dock and of a menu, its two edges eased; everything else rounded as today. Cheaper than a rounded corner: a span offset a row.
Deskbar and dock
The strip opens on the Kosmos plate with the four-point star; the dock is a cut bridge, and a running application is marked by a glint, amber for the one in front. A 9 px sprite each.
The three
Round, as today, in Starlight's colours: astral blue, lavender, and close in the flare; grey behind. As OUT.light draws them now: only the colours change.
Icons
Haiku's manner, kept and made the rule: free-standing objects, never on a tile.
Motion: unfurl
The plate arrives, the window unrolls beneath it in 150 ms; a close rolls back up into it. A clip, not a scale: only the rows revealed, nothing blended.

Observatory

Indigo Magic's manner: an instrument rather than a toy. The dimensional principle of ui.md 16.8b, kept literally.

Title
A bevelled bar, slate when behind and deep teal in front, a window-menu square at its left. A gradient a row and two 1 px edges.
Colour
Blue-grey slate everywhere, one teal. Nothing.
Material
No glass: raised, sunken and grooved, light from the upper left, a hard offset shadow. The cheapest of the three; no blur ever.
Deskbar and dock
A bevelled console strip with a Toolchest button; the dock a row of raised keys.
Icons
Line vectors in one colour each, as Indigo Magic's scaled ones were.
Motion: zoom rectangles
Six outlines from the dock to the window and back. Four thin rectangles a frame.
Risk
Diego chose flat looks (Endeavour, Plex) after Classic; this is Classic's road, and reads 1996.

Aurora

Every application a colour of its own, the way a planetarium labels a sky.

Title
A 3 px band in the application's hue across every window's top; the one in front is 4 px and glows. A 3 px line; the glow a 16 px falloff from shadow.c's table.
Colour
Near-black, with each application's hue - a new -- kosmos: hue line in its opening comment.
Material
Dark glass whose edge takes the front application's hue. Wallpaper glass; the hue is a tint.
Deskbar and dock
The strip's bottom edge glows the front application's hue; running marks are hue bars.
Icons
Line glyphs in the hue.
Motion: band sweep
The band sweeps across on focus and opening. A 3 px line.
Risk
Forty hues to choose and keep apart, and a band is a quieter mark than a plate - at a glance it can still read as "dark macOS".
