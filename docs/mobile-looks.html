BSky

The mode is a sky that fills the screen and changes with the day: a warm dusk while you focus, a night with a moon when you rest, a clear blue when you are out. The mode is a planet; what it allows are its moons, on glass.

  • The most alive and the most beautiful of the three
  • Kosmos is in its name: it is the one only this system would have
  • Rest dims to almost nothing

CCards

Each mode is a card in its own strong colour, and the others peek out behind it: swipe the card away to change mode, as if dealing from a deck. Dot-matrix numerals, chunky buttons with words on them, nothing small.

  • Playful and physical; you always know where you are
  • Changing mode is the most direct of the three
  • Bold; the loudest of the three
