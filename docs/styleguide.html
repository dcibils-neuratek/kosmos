The look of Kosmos

One page that says what every window is made of: its colours, its type, the space between things, and each widget - as the kit draws it today, and as proposed. Change this page until it is right; the kit follows it, one application at a time.

Roadmap item 5. The tab is already done - BeOS's, by default, since 19 September. The rest is proposed and nothing below is decided: every "proposed" is a question.

Colours

The BeOS palette, exactly as themes.lua carries it - R5's own values from Haiku's table. Two rules make it BeOS rather than any grey desktop: a button is lighter than the panel it sits on, and nothing is blue but the desktop and the focus ring.

What changes, widget by widget: more room inside each control, the default button said by weight and an outer rule instead of the ring, a tick in a checkbox rather than an x, lists with rows you can hit, headers set small and bold, menus that show their keys, and bars with rounded ends. The colours do not change - the BeOS palette above, in both columns.
