MathLab

Equations, and their graphs in two and three dimensions, with the processor's vector units doing the arithmetic — and saying how much faster that was. Drawn at the size the machine draws it before any of it is written (roadmap.md 4k). Diego: "a mathcad oriented app that we can do math equations and graphics using all the available simd features", starting with f(x) = x2.

What it is made of

WorksheetLua: each line parsed into an expression, and a function compiled into a short program of vector steps — load x, square, multiply, add — that the kit runs over every point at once
Maths KitC, use("/kits/math"): those programs over arrays, matrix multiply, 4 × 4 transforms, dot products; NEON on AArch64, SSE on x86-64, AVX2 when the kernel keeps its registers — each held to its one-at-a-time version on the Mac, as yuv.c is
2D graphAxes, grid and curves drawn into a surface in C; the readout under the pointer from the same numbers
3D graphThe surface's points turned by one 4 × 4 matrix, its faces coloured by height and painted back to front with their mesh, in C; drag to turn, wheel to zoom
BenchmarkA window of its own: the units this processor has, and each kind of work timed both ways, every answer checked first
File.math, plain text, one line an equation, in /home — the grey lines above are exactly what it holds
Window1180 by 700: the kit's header, the worksheet 380 wide, the graph, and the foot

For Diego to decide

  • A list of lines, as drawn, or a page where equations sit anywhere, as Mathcad places them. A list is simpler to type into and to keep in a file; a page is closer to Mathcad.
  • How equations are typed: x^2 and x*x both; 3x meaning three times x; pi and e by name.
  • Shown set, as drawn, with the typed text under each line — or set only, the typed text appearing when the line is being edited.
  • The 3D look: faces and mesh as in the MATLAB picture, drawn above; or without the mesh; or a wire mesh only.
  • What comes first after f(x) = x2: questions about a function (values, roots, maxima, integrals, as drawn), or more kinds of graph (parametric curves, contour maps, several surfaces).
