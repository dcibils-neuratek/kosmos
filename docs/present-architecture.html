About 3 ms of a 16.7 ms frame before T6. Push, Wipe, Move In and Reveal touch the screen once and are about half. Not on the path: setting, rasterising, scaling a picture, the disk, a payload of pixels - the next slide was drawn while this one was shown.

Nothing here has been measured in Write either, which draws its whole window on every key. Step P4 measures a keystroke in Write first, by stage and by what each allocates, and holds Present's to it - Groove went from 72 ms to 10.1 a frame by drawing only what changed.

A slide is a page of frames

Every thing on a slide is a small Write document set in its box by pageset; the slide is a page whose content is its frames, each at its place.

Lua decides, C draws

A transition is positions and an alpha in Lua and two million pixels in C; each call writes three orders past the two thousand a crossing needs.

Pictures never become strings

Inflated into a region, decoded from it, kept scaled at the sizes shown, copied into a saved deck as stored.
