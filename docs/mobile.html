What this phone believes

  • A mode is what you are doing, and it is the interface. Essential, Focus, Out, Rest, Open: each decides what is on the screen, who can reach you and what waits. The colour at the screen's edge says which, always.
  • Calm is the default; Open is a lease. Going calmer is one gesture. Opening everything asks for how long, and gives itself back when the time is up.
  • People come straight; everything else comes by post. Your circle reaches you in every mode but Rest's quietest hours. Mail, news and the rest are held and delivered together at the times you choose, as a list that ends.
  • Nothing is endless. No feed scrolls for ever; every list has a last line that says it is the last.
  • No red numbers. Something that has news for you glows softly. It never says how many.
  • Gestures first, and each edge means one thing. Left is people, top is the day, bottom is home and the modes.
  • Kept by the system, not by willpower. In Kosmos a program reaches only what it was handed (CLAUDE.md, capabilities). In Essential the browser is not hidden behind a tap - it is not handed the network. A mode is a set of capabilities, which no other phone can say.
