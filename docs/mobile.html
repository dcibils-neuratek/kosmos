What this phone believes

  • A mode is what you are doing, and it is the interface. Essential, Focus, Out, Rest, Open: each decides what is on the page, who can reach you and what waits. The page says it in a sentence, and the mode's colour inks its word.
  • Calm is the default; Open is a lease. Going calmer is one gesture. Opening everything asks for how long, and gives itself back when the time is up.
  • People come straight; everything else comes by post. Your circle reaches you in every mode, and calls in Rest's. Mail, news and the rest are held and delivered together at the times you choose, as a list that ends.
  • Nothing is endless. No feed scrolls for ever; every list has a last line that says it is the last.
  • No red numbers. Something new is a small inked mark beside its name. It never says how many.
  • Everything is one swipe away, and asks for how long. In from the right is every application. What the mode includes opens at once; what it leaves out asks for how long, and only that application is given the minutes - the rest of the phone stays where it was.
  • Words, not icons. A page reads like a page: things are named, and the phone does not try to be looked at.
  • Everything can be tapped; gestures are shortcuts. The foot of every page says where things are - People, The day, the mode, Everything - and an application has Home at its top. Each edge is the same thing as a shortcut: left is people, top is the day, right is everything, bottom is home and, held, the modes.
  • Kept by the system, not by willpower. In Kosmos a program reaches only what it was handed (CLAUDE.md, capabilities). In Essential the browser is not hidden behind a tap - it is not handed the network. A mode is a set of capabilities, which no other phone can say.
