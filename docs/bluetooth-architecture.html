Bluetooth - what it stands on

The app holds nothing: it asks the host stack through one door and draws the answer. The stack is a driver process of its own, in C - HCI, L2CAP, pairing, HID - and the USB driver only carries its packets, through two rings in a shared region. A key pressed on a Bluetooth keyboard (K) never comes near the window; the window (W) asks on its own clock.

K - a key pressed

  1. K1 The adapter's bulk IN completes an ACL packet; xhci writes it into the ring and sends "more" if the ring was empty.
  2. K2 The stack takes it from the ring: L2CAP's header off, the HID channel found, the report read.
  3. K3 Each key that changed to kosmos_key_push, as a USB keyboard's. Two processes, one copy; the radio's 7.5 to 30 ms dwarfs it.

W - the window

  1. W1 One request: the state, the adapter, a page of devices.
  2. W2 The stack answers from what it keeps; nothing waits on the radio.
  3. W3 The lists redrawn when they changed; a click painted before its reply.
