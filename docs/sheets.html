What it does, first

  • Sheets and their canvas: sheets as tabs; on each, tables, charts, text boxes, shapes and pictures placed with the pointer, moved, sized, brought forward and sent back, snapped to each other's edges.
  • Tables: header rows and columns that stay in view while a long table scrolls, footer rows for totals, a name, six looks, gridlines - and no row or column count compiled in. 100,000 rows scroll as 10 do.
  • Formulas: Excel's grammar and about eighty of its functions; references relative and absolute; references to another table by its name (Costs!E7), on this sheet or another; errors as values; each reference in its colour while it is typed, outlined on the canvas; quick sums from the tools' Formula, and the foot summing whatever is chosen.
  • Formats: Automatic, Number, Currency, Percentage, Scientific, Text, Date and Time - the Format panel's Cell; a cell's face, size, colour, fill and alignment - its Text, as Write's.
  • Sort and filter: Organize's two parts, by one or more columns and by rules.
  • Charts from cells: column, bar, line, pie, area and scatter, drawn again when a number they read changes.
  • Files: .sheets, its own; CSV in and out; Excel's XLSX out and in; PDF out, fitted to the page's width - from Export.

What is yours to decide

1. The arithmetic

Binary doubles, as Excel and Numbers keep them - what is shown and what is compared rounded to fifteen digits, ROUND for an exact cent - or decimal arithmetic, exact in cents and different from the Excel a sheet is exported to.

Doubles: every function is defined over them, an XLSX holds them, and a number computes the same at both ends.

2. How a reference names another table

Costs!E7, a table's name unique in the document - or Numbers' Q3 2026::Costs::E7.

The table's name: a table moves between sheets without breaking a formula, and each table becomes an Excel worksheet of the same name, so nothing is renamed.

3. Opening Excel's files

XLSX out only, as asked, or in as well, in the first version.

In as well, after out: a worksheet's cells are not a page's layout, and people's spreadsheets arrive as XLSX.

4. How numbers and dates are written

1,234.56 and day first (5 October 2026) to begin with, the document recording it - or the decimal comma from the first day.

The point and day first, recorded, so that a region chosen later is a setting rather than a format change.

5. The clipboard

It holds 1,900 bytes, which a range of cells soon outgrows: within Sheets nothing is lost, to another application the text is cut short and said to be. A clipboard in a region is the window manager's change.

Later, as its own step: every application gains from it.
