What it does

It appears when a level changes, and only then. A key, or dragging the knob. It stays for two seconds after the last change and fades; the × that shows on hover takes it away at once.
One panel for both, titled by what it controls - Display with suns, Sound with speakers. Muted, the speaker is struck through and the bar goes grey, keeping its level, so unmuting comes back where it was.
Drawn by the window manager, over everything, because the keys are the whole system's and not any one application's - the same place the Super key is handled today. Nothing about it waits on the thing it controls: the panel moves at the moment of the key, from what it already knows.
The ThinkPad decides how many real steps brightness has, and the DSDT will say - its list of backlight levels. Sixteen here is a stand-in until it is read off the machine.
