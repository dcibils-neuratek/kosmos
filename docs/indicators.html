Network and USB in the Deskbar

Two icons beside the volume and the battery. One says whether the adapter is there and the network is on; the other lists everything on the USB ports and what Kosmos does with each. Both only report what the drivers already know, so neither can show something that isn't true.

The mouse, the stick, the camera, the Synaptics device and the Intel one are exactly what Kosmos's USB driver named on the ThinkPad (usb.md 4 and 5), with the ports and speeds it gave. The adapter's line is what it read from your dongle on the Mac. The address, the gateway and the traffic are examples: nothing gets an address until the frames reach the network stack (roadmap.md 5m-d).

The network icon's four states

Each state is a step the driver has actually reached, so the icon never claims more than it knows. Greyed out, then a dot that changes colour: that is all the bar shows, which keeps it steady. The speed is in the panel.

No adapter

Nothing plugged in that a driver here can drive. The icon stays, greyed out, so "no network" is something the bar says at a glance; the panel says "no adapter".

The USB list

Every device on every port, grouped by controller. For each: the name it gave itself, vendor and product, the port and the speed, and on the right, what Kosmos does with it, in one of three colours:

driven Kosmos uses it, and says for what: the pointer, /home, a pad's buttons, Ethernet. not driven yet Kosmos knows what it is but doesn't use it yet, as with the adapter tonight. no driver no driver here for it, with its class so you can see what kind of device it is.

The count on the icon is every device, including the ones inside the machine, and the panel's first line splits it into driven and not. It changes as something is plugged in or pulled out.
