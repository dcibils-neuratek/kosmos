Browser, on WebKit

Kosmos's own browser, as current as Safari: WebKit underneath, drawn with Skia, each tab's page in a process of its own. The window is Kosmos's - its band, its menus, its keys - and the page is the web as Safari shows it. Drawn before it is built, 11 November 2026.

Saturn

Saturn is the sixth planet from the Sun and the second largest in the Solar System, after Jupiter. It is a gas giant with an average radius of about nine and a half times that of Earth, and only one eighth of its average density.

Its interior is most likely made of a core of iron, nickel and rock, surrounded by a deep layer of metallic hydrogen, an intermediate layer of liquid hydrogen and helium, and a gaseous outer layer. Its pale yellow hue comes from ammonia crystals in its upper atmosphere.

Its ring system is made mostly of ice particles, with a smaller amount of rocky debris and dust.

The band is Kosmos's, with the browser's menus; tabs below it, then back, forward and reload, the address field, a star for bookmarks, downloads (a thin bar while one runs) and the rest. Super with a number goes to a tab, Control+T opens one, Control+L is the address field. A link under the pointer shows its address at the bottom left. The page is drawn by WebKit's web process into a region and shown as it is - fonts, layout and colours are the site's.

Each tab's page runs in a process of its own. When one ends - a fault, or the page itself - only its tab says so; the browser and the other tabs go on. A page that stops answering for several seconds offers Wait or Stop the page in the same place.
