The busiest path - a film playing under the dock, with Glass on

  1. The film's window commits a frame; its rectangle is damage.
  2. The damage reaches the dock's blur margin, so the whole dock is damage.
  3. The scene composes the layers below the dock into the back buffer, as now.
  4. That rectangle, plus the blur's reach, is blurred at a quarter size from the back buffer.
  5. The dock's tint and its cells are blended over it; the screen is flushed.

About 0.5-0.6 ms a frame on the M700 (estimate, 3.4 ns a composed pixel measured, 18.657); a still desktop pays nothing. Measured with frames before G2 lands.
