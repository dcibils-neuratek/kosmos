Four looks, one layout

You pick a look, not its parts. Each look is a finished design: its colours, its fonts and its Deskbar, made together. All four sit on the same fixed layout, so a window looks different in each and behaves the same in all of them.

The fixed layout all four share

These sizes are the kit's, not a look's, and they don't change when a look does. The fonts in every look are chosen so they fit inside them. That's what makes a look safe to switch: no window can move or break.

whatsizewhat fits in it
a list or menu row24 pxPlex Sans 16 (21 px tall), 8 px in from the left
a button28 pxPlex Sans 16, 16 px either side
a text field26 pxPlex Sans 16, 8 px in
a window's tab20 pxPlex Sans Condensed SemiBold 14
the terminal's line24 pxPlex Mono 14
between widgets, and from a window's edge12 px—
the Deskbar36 px32-pixel icons, Plex Sans 16

Text longer than its box is cut there, never spilling into the widget beside it: the third row above ends in "…" when the list is narrow.

For you to choose

  1. Are these the looks? All four, three of them, or one to change.
  2. What else stays in Appearance: the wallpaper (your pictures, or the look's plain desk), and the Deskbar's height (36, 44, 52). My recommendation is to keep both and nothing else.
  3. The default on a machine nobody has set up: Plex, I'd suggest.
