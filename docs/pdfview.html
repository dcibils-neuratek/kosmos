PDF Viewer, made again

The reader from the first builds - a page at a time, no menu, a window that does not resize - as a reader of today: a menu, zoom, the whole document scrolled, pages and contents in a sidebar, search, the window resizable, and every document opened where it was left. Drawn before it is built.

Diego, 10 October 2026: "we need to revisit our pdf reader which hasnt been updated since the first kosmos builds", "it needs a menu, zoom, better navigation, window resizing, etc".

As it opens: the document scrolled whole, its pages beside it

The menu bar under the header - File, Edit, View, Go - as every Kosmos window with a menu has it. In the header: the page as a number to type into and its count, back and forward by a page, the zoom with its levels a click away, the sidebar shown or hidden, and Find. The pages are one column, scrolled with the wheel, the keys or the scroll bar, each with a gap of desk between; the sidebar's thumbnails follow, the page in view ringed. The foot says the zoom and where the document came from.

The View menu, and the zoom's levels

Fit width - the default - lays the pages as wide as the window and follows it as the window is resized; Fit page a whole page in view; Actual size a page at its printed size at the screen's density; and steps between 50% and 400%. One page at a time is there for a document read as slides. The sidebar shows the pages, or the document's own contents when it has them.

Find

Typed into the header's field, every match found through the whole document and marked, the current one stronger; Return and Shift-Return go to the next and the one before, and the sidebar lists the matches by page with a few words round each. A document whose text cannot be read as letters - a scan - says so rather than finding nothing.

Contents, and Go to page

The sidebar's other tab, Contents, is the document's own outline when it has one - its books and chapters - and the part being read lit. Go to page (Command-G, or a click on the number in the header) is a small sheet that takes a number.

A narrow window

Resized, the pages are laid out again at the window's width and nothing is cut: the header keeps the page, the zoom and Find, and puts the rest in the menu; the sidebar is hidden below a width and comes back when there is room.

What it does

  • Opens a PDF from Tracker, the Open window, or the File menu's recent documents.
  • Shows the whole document as one column of pages, scrolled; or one page at a time.
  • Zooms: fit width, fit page, actual size, and steps between 50% and 400%; Command-plus and -minus; pinching where there is a trackpad.
  • Goes: next and previous page, first and last, a page by number, back to where it was before a jump.
  • Shows its pages as thumbnails, and its contents when it has an outline.
  • Finds text through the whole document, every match marked.
  • Shows the pictures in its pages, which the reader of today leaves blank.
  • Copies selected text.
  • Resizes, laying its pages out again.
  • Opens each document where it was left: its page, its zoom.
  • Later, not in the first version: printing, which Kosmos does not do yet; a PDF's forms and annotations; two pages side by side.
