WebKit on Kosmos - what it stands on

WebKit, after its PlayStation and Windows ports: Skia on the processor, curl, JavaScriptCore with no JIT, no GLib and no GPU process. Its three processes are one C++ image that Kosmos spawns with a role each; they talk over endpoints, and pixels, network bodies and video frames cross in regions. POSIX lives inside those processes and nowhere else.

W - a scroll on a heavy page

  1. W1 The wheel reaches the Browser window; it becomes a message to the UI process.
  2. W2 The UI process scrolls the tiles it already has - the screen moves at once.
  3. W3 It asks the tab's web process for the tiles newly in view.
  4. W4 Layout if needed, then Skia paints them into the shared region.
  5. W5 "These tiles changed"; the surface is composed with the desktop.
