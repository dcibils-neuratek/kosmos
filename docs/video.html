Video

A window that plays an MP4: H.264 pictures and AAC sound, from a file in /home or on a stick. Music's sibling - the same dark player, the same engine underneath, the same keys.

A drawing, not a program: nothing here is built yet except the part that reads the file's index, mp4.lua, which read the facts below from Diego's clip. The pictures come from a decoder not chosen yet - FFmpeg's H.264 and AAC decoders are proposed (roadmap.md 4e).

The picture at its own size, 640 by 360, with the controls under it rather than over it - the film is never covered. The menu bar is the window manager's, drawn above a window that draws its own pixels, as the Super Nintendo's is. The window's tab says the film's name.
