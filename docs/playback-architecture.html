The Playback Kit - what it stands on

A song's or a film's sound plays from a thread of its own, in C and in the audio band. The window only reads the file - seconds ahead, into an input ring - and asks where the sound is. Neither a picture's decode, a garbage collection, nor a busy machine stands between a frame and the speaker any more; and nothing on the period path allocates.

P - a period of sound

  1. P1 The thread takes the next frame from the input ring - seconds of them are there.
  2. P2 Decoded into the kit's own buffer: AAC 1024 samples a channel, MP3 1152.
  3. P3 Converted to the device's rate and channels, the resampler's phase carried.
  4. P4 Written into the stream's next slot in place and published; 172 a second.
  5. P5 The audio server mixes it to the device. No Lua, no string, no message.

W - the window

  1. W1 On its tick: if the input ring has room, read the next piece of the file and hand its frames over.
  2. W2 position() from the stream's ring: frames out of the speaker.
  3. W3 Play, pause, seek and volume as commands; a seek a new stream and a new generation.
