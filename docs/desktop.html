The eight, larger

What the page settles

Colour is 5zk’s and this page only wears it. What eight windows together decide is shape, and it comes out as four kinds:

KindWhat it looks likeWhich ones
A list with places Sidebar, a header of back / where / two or three words, a white view Tracker, Preferences, Music, Scheduler
A table No sidebar. A header of what it is doing, columns, and a foot that counts Processes
A canvas Tools down the left as a strip, the work on white, nothing above it but the title Paint, Photo
A page of text The text is the window. One header, and the chrome disappears while typing Editor, Terminal

Three controls is the rule the header follows. Tracker has Find, New, View and ...; Preferences has none at all, because a sidebar and rows are the whole of it; Paint has its tools in a strip rather than a bar, because a tool is chosen far more often than a menu is opened. When a window wants a fourth button it wants a ... instead.

And what it deliberately keeps

The Deskbar is the tab colour and the desktop is Tracker with --backdrop; replicants still live in the bar, the right button still reaches the application, and an icon still stays where it is dragged. Those are how this desktop works, and a screenshot is not asking about them (roadmap.md 5zi).
