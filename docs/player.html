Music, the next one

A library player on iTunes's ground: songs, albums, artists, playlists and smart playlists, Up Next, a mini player. It plays FLAC, ALAC, AAC, MP3, WAV, AIFF, Vorbis and Opus at the file's own resolution, and always says honestly what reached the speaker. It is drawn in Astra's look, as Music was agreed on 11 October (docs/music.html), and this design is that one's next version (docs/player.md, decision 1). Every song, album and artist here is made up.

Diego, 22 November 2026: "design and build a new audio player app inspired in itunes and will hires audio support with flac and mp4, m4a, mp3, wav, etc", "we want the best audio player", and of the EDIFIER M60: "so we can play flac audio file sin full resolution", "in bitrate and all".

1. Songs, as it opens: a 96/24 FLAC playing, bit-perfect

What is playing stays on top. Beside the song's name is what reached the speaker. Green means every bit got there. That is not the player's claim: the audio server says it, per stream, because only it knows whether anything was mixed in, scaled or resampled. The list sorts by any column. Format and Quality are columns of their own, and the moving bars mark the song playing. Smart playlists (marked ✱) sit in the sidebar with the ordinary ones.

2. The signal path, one click on the badge

On the left, the same song alone at full volume, with every stage green. On the right, a 44.1 kHz ALAC, the volume at 70%, and a notification's sound arriving. Each stage that changed the bits is marked, and says what it did and what to do instead. The last line chooses the output, as Mixer does (usbaudio.md U5).

3. Albums

Every album as its cover. The cover comes from the file's own picture (ID3, FLAC's picture block, an M4A's covr) or a cover.jpg beside the songs. An album with none gets one drawn from its name. 96/24 on a cover marks a hi-res album. A compilation is one album, not twenty. Sort by artist, title, year or date added.

4. An album, opened

The cover large, the facts beneath it, Play and Shuffle, and its songs by disc and track. It plays gaplessly: the second song starts at the sample the first one ends. ‹ Albums, or Escape, goes back.

5. Artists

Each artist, by album artist where the files name one, with their albums as covers and their most played songs. A face is drawn from the name's letters. There are no photographs from the internet in tier 1.

6. Search, as you type

Across title, artist, album, album artist, composer and genre, grouped as Songs, Albums and Artists, with the match marked. It is a scan of the library in memory, a few milliseconds over ten thousand songs.

7. The column browser and Up Next

Control B shows iTunes's three columns, Genre, Artist and Album, over the song list. Three clicks narrow ten thousand songs. Up Next is the queue made visible. Play next and Play later come from a right-click, a song is dragged to reorder it, and History lies beneath. Editing the queue never interrupts the song playing.

8. A smart playlist

Rules over any field, including the ones a hi-res library wants: sample rate, bit depth and format. They can be all or any, with a limit, and they update live as the library changes. Five ship ready-made: Recently added, Most played, Top rated, Hi-Res and Never played. A play counts when a song finishes, as iTunes counted.

9. The mini player and the Deskbar

The mini player keeps the cover, the song, the transport and the bar, and it can be kept on top. The Deskbar's replicant gives the same three buttons in the bar. Clicking the song opens the player.
