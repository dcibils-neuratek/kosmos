7. Making the car: wheels, lamps and the picture

The wheels and the details that make it a car, and then its picture.

The wheels

  • 13. Add a Cylinder. In its Data tab: Radius 0.38, Depth 0.3, and Shading Smooth, so it renders perfectly round.
  • 14. In the Object tab: Rotation 90, 0, 0 stands it on its edge, and Location 1.35, 0.92, 0.38 puts it at the front left.
  • 15. Its material: Plastic, the black swatch, Roughness 0.9, and the Noise texture with its scale at 40 - rubber.
  • 16. Press Shift D, Esc, and type the copy's Location: 1.35, -0.92, 0.38. Twice more for -1.35, 0.92 and -1.35, -0.92. Four wheels.
  • 17. The hubs: add a cylinder of Radius 0.2 and Depth 0.32 - a little wider than the tyre, so it shows - smooth, turned 90, 0, 0, at the first wheel's location. Make it Metal with the white swatch and Roughness 0.4: brushed aluminium. Copy it to the other three wheels as you did the tyres.

Press Home and turn around your car. It is a car already.

Lamps that glow

  • 18. Headlights: add a UV Sphere of Radius 0.12, smooth. Give it the Scale 0.6, 1, 1 - flattened front to back - and the Location 2.08, 0.6, 0.72. Its material: the Light preset, Base colour fff3c4. Copy it to 2.08, -0.6, 0.72.
  • 19. Tail lights: a cube of Size 0.04, 0.36, 0.1 at -2.11, 0.6, 0.78, with the Light preset, colour ff2a1a and Emission 3. Copy it to -2.11, -0.6, 0.78.
  • 20. Bumpers: a cube of Size 0.14, 1.84, 0.2 at 2.16, 0, 0.44, Plastic, the black swatch, Roughness 0.6; a copy at -2.16, 0, 0.44.

The finished car, twenty-one objects with the ground, the lamp and the camera, after Home.

The picture

  • 21. Click Rendered. The view goes grainy, then clears as Cafesa3D adds sample after sample: the red shines, the lamp's highlight sits on the bonnet, and the car's shadow falls soft on the asphalt.
  • 22. Press F12. A Render window opens and takes the picture through the camera, counting its samples as it goes. Render again starts it afresh after a change.

What F12 makes of it: 256 samples, 640 by 360 - the red paint catching the lamp, the headlights glowing, the shadow soft on the asphalt.

Where to go from here

  • See what the sky does: in the World tab set Strength to 0 and watch the Rendered view. Every side the lamp does not reach goes black. Then put it back.
  • Open the sample car from the dots menu and look at it beside yours: the same shapes, with a lofted body and lathed tyres. Choose its parts and read their tabs - its paint, its tinted glasshouse, its brake calipers.
  • Add a brick wall behind your car: a cube 16 long, 0.3 thick and 4 high, with the Brick texture.
  • Try other paint: the blue swatch, or Metal with the yellow swatch for a gold show car.
