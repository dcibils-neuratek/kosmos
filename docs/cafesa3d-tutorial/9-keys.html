9. Keys at a glance

Looking around

  • Drag in the view - turn around.
  • Shift drag - slide the view.
  • Wheel - closer and further.
  • 1 3 7 - front, right, top; with Shift, back, left, bottom.
  • Home - everything in view.
  • Z - Solid and Wireframe.

Objects

  • Click - choose an object; click the sky to choose nothing.
  • Shift A - add an object.
  • Shift D - copy the chosen object; Esc leaves the copy where the original is.
  • X - delete, asking first; Delete - delete now.
  • Ctrl Z - undo; Ctrl Shift Z - redo.

Moving, turning, sizing

  • G R S - move, rotate, scale with the mouse, no button held.
  • X Y Z while doing it - hold to that axis; Shift X - to the other two.
  • A number while doing it - that exact amount; - for negative.
  • Click or Return - put it down; Esc or right click - put it back.

Typing into a box

  • Click a box - type into it; drag across it - slide its number.
  • Return - keep; Esc - leave as it was; Tab - keep, and on to the next box.
  • A colour is typed as six characters, rrggbb, without the #.

Pictures

  • F12 - render through the camera.
  • F1 - this tutorial.
