Flight - what it stands on

A flight simulator over a real city: elevation and satellite tiles fetched by the tiles server Maps already uses, decoded by gfx, turned into a terrain by the 3D Kit and drawn by Vulkan - anv on the M700's GPU, lavapipe where there is none. Flight itself is Lua: flying, the camera and the panel - the thinnest layer there is.

F - a frame, 16.7 ms

  1. F1 flight.lua: keys, forces, attitude, the camera - about 0.2 ms.
  2. F2 The terrain chooses ~150 chunks and uploads a few arrived tiles - about 0.5 ms.
  3. F3 The command buffer recorded: chunks, the aircraft, sky, instruments - 0.5 to 1 ms.
  4. F4 The GPU draws: depth, terrain, shadows, haze, MSAA - 5 to 8 ms on the HD 530.
  5. F5 To the screen: one copy into the window until G9 - measured, and named in the panel.

Estimates, labelled as estimates; the graphics panel reads the real ones. Nothing on this path decodes, reads the disk or waits on the network.

T - a tile's way in

  1. T1 The terrain wants the tiles around the camera: WANT, a list of numbers, to tiles.
  2. T2 tiles fetches each and writes it to the cache; ARRIVED says which.
  3. T3 The decoding thread reads it, decodes it - JPEG, or PNG to heights - and puts it on the ring for the next frame.
