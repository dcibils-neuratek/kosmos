Rendering modes

How the desktop is drawn, chosen on Preferences' Displays page and tried at once: every step measured on the M700, from the plain framebuffer to GPU composing, each saying what it does, who does each stage of a frame, what it costs in GPU memory and what it measures - kept only when you say so.

The Displays page, a mode just chosen

Each mode in a row: its name; a sentence of what it does; a strip of the six stages a frame goes through - the window draws, the desktop is described, the frame composed, copied to the screen, shown, and the pointer - each coloured by who does it, as docs/composing.png draws them; and what it costs in GPU memory at this screen's size. Choosing one switches the desktop at once, and the bar at the bottom counts down: nothing answered in 15 seconds goes back to the mode before, so a mode that breaks the screen undoes itself. Under the list, what the mode in use is doing now, a second at a time, so two modes can be compared on this machine. A mode this machine cannot have is greyed with its reason; GPU composing until step 7 is built.
