To decide

Each is drawn above as recommended. Answer by number.

  1. Line icons at 19 pixels, in the bar's text colour.

    They follow the look: dark on Plex's yellow and on the blue, dark on Plex Night's amber. 15 reads as small beside the clock; 23 is as loud as today's pictures.

  2. The battery keeps its percentage, and the bolt replaces the word "charging".

    A glyph alone says roughly how full; the number is what a person looks for.

  3. Network: the Ethernet port for a wired card, Wi-Fi's arcs once there is Wi-Fi, and the crossed arcs when there is no network at all — where today leaves the picture out.

    A missing icon says nothing; the crossed one says the machine is offline.

  4. Muted sound shows the speaker crossed, which today cannot show at all.
  5. The Kosmos button keeps its colour picture.

    It is the system's mark and the menu's button, not an indicator.
