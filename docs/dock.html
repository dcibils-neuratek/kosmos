A dock at the bottom, as Googlebook has it

A second way to wear Kosmos: the bar moved to the bottom and centred as a dock, a launcher above it, quick settings at the top right, and windows and panels in the rounded, dark-blue language of Google's Googlebook. An Appearance setting beside the BeOS-style bar, which stays exactly as it is. Nothing here is built.

"I dont want to replace the current BeOS inspired one, but i would like to have an appearance setting to place the taskbar on the bottom center and replicate as mich as possible the design language of googlebook." - Diego, 3 October

What is yours to decide

1. One setting, or two?

Where the bar is, and what windows look like, could be one choice ("Googlebook") or two that mix - the dock with Plex, or the top bar with the new colours.

Two, as drawn in the window above: The bar (Top / Bottom, centred) and Look, with a new look beside Plex, Classic and Studio. Choosing the dock offers the new look the first time; nothing changes without being asked.

2. The dock: floating, or the whole width?

Googlebook shows both: a bar across the bottom with the icons centred in it, and on the newer machines a rounded dock floating above the edge.

Floating, as drawn: it is the modern half of the picture, and the space beside it stays the desktop's.

3. The time and the indicators: a strip at the top, or in the dock?

Googlebook keeps a thin strip across the top - the time on the left, the indicators on the right, quick settings under them - and the dock holds only applications.

The strip, as drawn: transparent over the wallpaper, so it costs almost nothing of the screen, and the dock stays the length of what is pinned.

4. Its name

A look in Kosmos should not carry another company's product name. Working name above: Night for the look, dock for the bar's place.

5. Widgets on the desktop - decided: not now

Googlebook's desktop carries cards - the time, what is playing, a watchlist. Diego, 3 October: "i dont care for widgets for now". So the desktop is the wallpaper, its icons and the windows; the roadmap's desklets stay where they were.

What it would take

  • A new look file, beside Plex's: the night surfaces, the pale blue accent, corners of 18 for a window and 28 for a panel, tiles as pills.
  • The Deskbar learning a second layout: the same Deskbar - the Kosmos menu, the running windows, pinned launchers - drawn as a dock, and the strip at the top with the time and indicators.
  • The window manager's work area at the bottom: today it keeps the top for the bar (reserved_top); with the dock it keeps a strip at the top and the dock's height at the bottom, so a maximised window sits between them.
  • The launcher: Open (launchpad) drawn as Googlebook's grid above the dock, with its search.
  • Quick settings: new, from what exists - the volume, the brightness on a ThinkPad, the network's address, the servers, restart and shut down.
  • The window's title bar: the icon and name on the left, the three on the right - the window manager's job, as Plex's header bars are now.
