Mixer, with a choice of device

Mixer as it is now, plus an Output and an Input chooser, each device's format and what it can do, and what happens when a USB speaker comes and goes. Drawn on 22 November 2026, before any of it is built; docs/usbaudio.md has what it is made of. For Diego to change until it is right.

Diego, 8 October: "the mixer app that shows volumes should have a sound out and in device selector. we need to know which device is selected", "also what bitrate, hz and channels the device has", "16 bit, 24 bit, etc".

The window, playing through the Edifier speakers

Output is the device that plays, with how it is connected beneath its name. The three tiles are what it is running at now; beneath each, what else it offers. Master and the Playing list are as Mixer has them today: each program's own level, mixed before the device. Input is the device that records, with a level meter, the first proof it hears; programs that record come at U9.

Choosing the output

Every output, the machine's own first. Choosing one moves every program's sound there at once, mid-song; nothing that plays has to know. The tick is the one playing.

The format, where a device offers more than one

A click on a tile lists what the device offers for it. The default is the best it offers that Kosmos mixes without loss; a DAC that takes 24 bits at 96 kHz shows both, and is held there. The mix itself is at 44.1 kHz and converted at the device (usbaudio.md, decision 1), which the line under the tiles says when it happens.

Plugged in, and pulled out

Plugging a speaker in names it, and, if it was the one chosen last, sound goes back to it by itself (decision 3). Pulling it out moves the sound to the machine's own device at once and says so. The speakers' own volume buttons move Master, as the keyboard's volume keys do.

Left out, on purpose

  • Several outputs at once. One plays; the choice moves it.
  • An equaliser. Its own roadmap entry, and it belongs to the mix, not to a device.
  • A per-program device. Every program plays on the one chosen. If a program ever needs its own, it is a field in its stream, not a second mixer.
