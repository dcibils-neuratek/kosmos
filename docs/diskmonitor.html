Disk Monitor

Every drive this machine is using - the disk /Home is on, a stick, a share on another computer, the memory /Temporary is kept in - with what each is reading and writing now and over the last minute, how long it takes to answer, the totals, and which programs are doing it.

As it opens

Every drive on the left, each drawn as what it is - an SSD, a USB stick, a share on another computer, memory - and each with its reading and writing now and a small graph of the minute - reading in blue, writing in orange - the same two colours as Network Monitor, coming in and going out, the kit's own. The chosen one on the right: what it is and where it is mounted, its rates now, the minute's graph with reading above the line and writing below, then how long a request takes - its average over the last second, reading and writing apart - and how often the cache answered without asking the drive; the totals since the machine started; and the programs reading and writing it now, the busiest first, with the file each touched last.
