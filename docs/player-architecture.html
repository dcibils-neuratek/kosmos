Music, the next one - what it stands on

A library player is the thinnest layer: the library's facts and the window in Lua, every sample in C. The Media Kit decodes, the Playback Kit plays a queue joined sample for sample, and the audio server mixes in 32 bits at the output's rate and reports whether every bit reached the speaker. Nothing here is a second sound path; the Library kit is new, and the rest grow.

H - a 96/24 FLAC, end to end

  1. H1 The window hands the queue a list of paths and says play; it touches no sample.
  2. H2 The feeder reads /Home, demuxes, decodes with FFmpeg's FLAC to int32 - about 450 KB/s in, 768 KB/s out.
  3. H3 Trimmed and joined; a boundary record between songs, so the title changes at the right sample.
  4. H4 Eight seconds ahead in the input ring; the AUDIO thread copies a period into the stream opened at 96/24 - no conversion.
  5. H5 One stream, gain and master at unity, no equaliser: the mix is the samples unchanged, exact = 1.
  6. H6 576 bytes a millisecond to the M60's setting 1.2. The badge reads bit-perfect because the server said so.
