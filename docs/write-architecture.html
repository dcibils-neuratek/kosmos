A - a key that ends a word

  1. A1 textedit: a new body, every other paragraph shared.
  2. A2 pageset sets that paragraph again, and the next only as far as the lines come back into step.
  3. A3 the Spelling Kit checks the word just finished.
  4. A4 pagedraw draws the pages on the screen.

Measured in a 100-page document at O1, by stage and by what it allocates. Footnotes (R4) are the one addition that can push A2 further, and are held to that number.

The steps

  1. R1 find, spelling, word count, the editing keys, recent documents, recovery, templates
  2. R2 styles changed and added, character styles, links, headers and footers
  3. R3 DOCX, text and Markdown opened; HTML and Markdown out
  4. R4 the outline, contents, footnotes, sections, columns
