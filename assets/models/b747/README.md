- `boeing_747.glb`: 116644 bytes, SHA-256 361d9a1be6b520c56e1e26a28e367139ef50e07270de180858e7f44a8f508d04
- glTF 2.0, written by obj2gltf; one mesh, 1,904 triangles in three
  materials (white, grey, and the cockpit's glass at 40%), no textures,
  no moving parts. It is drawn in a frame of its own, about 1.7 units
  long: `Aircraft/b747.lua`'s `pose` turns and scales it to Flight's.

Flight's second aircraft (`docs/flight.md` F12b). The terms are in
