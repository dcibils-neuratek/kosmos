# assets/wallpapers

The desktop's pictures, carried in the image: twelve abstract pictures
chosen by Diego on 12 October 2026 - "replace the current wallpapers bundled
in kosmos with the ones in kosmos-abastract-wallpapers.zip", "from now on the
bundled system available wallpapers are these new ones". Appearance lists
them beside any picture in `/Home`, and the window manager draws them as it
draws those. They replace the 24 Unsplash photographs carried since 18
September.

**As he gave them, byte for byte, at their own resolutions** - "i want the
wallpapers to maintain its current resolution", "as my monitor is a ultra
wide 3440 monitor". Most are 3440x1440, his screen's; three are 3840x2160,
one 3840x1600 and one 5120x1440. Nothing was scaled or re-encoded, so
`CLAUDE.md`'s rule for vendored data holds without the exception the
photographs needed; the only change is each file's name, from a number to
what it shows, which is what the Wallpaper row reads
(`settings.wallpaper_name`). 7.9 MB for the twelve.

**In `FULL=1` images only** - `make qemu`, a stick, `make test`'s machines -
and not in `FULL=0` or the test and bench images, as before.

**Where they came from**: WallpaperAccess (wallpaperaccess.com), for
personal use - Diego, 12 October 2026: "they are from WallpaperAccess and
are ok for personal use". The numbers they arrived named by are kept below,
beside each name, as the site's ids for them.
